Run the GTM Growth exercise from `~/.claude/skills/gtm-launch/SKILL.md` (Stage 8: Growth).

1. Understand the product model — is it self-serve, sales-led, or hybrid? Ask if unclear.
2. Identify which growth loop type fits best (viral, content, or product-led sales)
3. Map the current activation flow and identify the aha moment
4. Design a referral loop with trigger, share mechanism, incentive, and reward structure
5. Evaluate whether a free tool / engineering-as-marketing play makes sense
6. Produce a 30-day growth sprint plan with specific actions, owners, and success metrics
7. Define the activation metric and time-to-value target

Bias toward loops that compound, not one-shot campaigns. A founder's time is the scarcest resource — prioritize by leverage.
