---
name: geo audit
description: Audit an entire site's GEO readiness across multiple pages
---

Run the GEO Site Audit from `~/.claude/skills/geo/SKILL.md`.

1. Identify high-value pages on the site (informational, comparison, and guide content)
   - Read sitemap, crawl key pages, or ask the user which pages to audit
2. Score each page using the GEO scoring rubric
3. Produce the Site Audit Summary:
   - Total pages audited
   - Average GEO score
   - Distribution (how many pages in each score band)
   - Category averages (Structure, Authority, Extractability, Technical)
4. List top issues by frequency across all pages
5. Create a priority fix matrix (impact vs effort)
6. Deep-dive the top 5 highest-opportunity pages (high traffic potential + low score)

For sites with 10+ pages, focus on the most important ones first and offer to continue with the rest.
